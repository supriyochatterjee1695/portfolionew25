Select Your Question

Are two groups different? T-Test

Compare average performance between two groups

Example: Is treatment A better than treatment B?

Did something change before and after? Paired T-Test

Test if there's a difference before and after an intervention

Example: Did test scores improve after training?

Which group is best among many? ANOVA

Compare average performance across 3 or more groups

Example: Which marketing channel performs best (Email, Social, Ads)?

Are two things related? Correlation

Test if two variables move together

Example: Does study time correlate with exam scores?

Are success rates different? Z-Test

Compare percentages or proportions between groups

Example: Do conversion rates differ between versions?
